PATRICK KAFATHE — PORTFOLIO
===========================

1. UPLOAD the whole folder to your hosting (public_html or a sub-folder).
2. Open  /admin/index.html  — default password: kafathe2026  (change it under Settings).
3. Edit your content in the admin panel, then click "Export site.json" and replace data/site.json on the hosting
   so everyone sees your changes.

CLIENT REVIEWS (needs PHP hosting — almost every shared host has it)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Open api/config.php and replace 'admin_token' with your own long private phrase.
- Make sure the folders  api/store  and  api/uploads  are writable (permission 755 or 775).
- Clients write reviews on the site (with the AI helper). They stay hidden until you approve them in
  Admin > Reviews (enter the same token there). You also get an email at 'notify_email'.
- No PHP? Reviews are sent to you on WhatsApp and you add them by hand in Admin > Reviews.

PRICE LIST
----------
The "Check website prices" button downloads assets/docs/MK-Technologies-Website-Packages.pdf.
To update the prices, replace that PDF and keep the same file name.
